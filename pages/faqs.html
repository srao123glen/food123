What is Food123?

Food123 is an educational platform founded by Dr. Srinivasa K. Rao to promote healthy eating and lifestyle habits through engaging tools, books, games, and projects.

Who is Dr. Srinivasa K. Rao?

Dr. Rao is a New York–based biomedical scientist and entrepreneur with decades of research and teaching experience in biology, nutrition, and public health. He has authored books, created educational tools, and developed health-focused projects.

Are your resources suitable for schools?

Yes! Our products and books are widely used by schools, teachers, and parents to introduce kids to nutrition and health in fun, engaging ways.

What age groups are your books and games for?

Our resources are designed for children, parents, and educators. Products like Food for Health Coloring Book and Food Rhymes for Health are ideal for kids, while 100 Food Rules and puzzles work for families, schools, and health professionals.

Where can I buy your products?

You can purchase directly through our website’s [Products Page]. Some titles are also available on platforms like Amazon (link if applicable).

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we offer international shipping. Delivery time and cost vary depending on your location.

Are your products available in digital format?

Some resources, like sample charts or e-books, may be available digitally. Details will be mentioned on each product page.
